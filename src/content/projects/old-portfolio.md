## What it was

A single long page with five sections: About, Projects, Skills & tools, Experience and Contact. Behind the intro card sits a 3D room built with three.js: a desk, a glowing PC, a lamp and a 3D version of me at the keyboard.

## The idea

Scrolling doesn't just move the page. The camera pushes in over my shoulder until the monitor fills the screen, and the editor on it shows `checkout.spec.js` with one failing test. Only then does the page move on to the projects.

## Why it's still online

This site replaced it. The old one stays online as a snapshot of where I started.
