# Product

**What it is:** the personal portfolio of Rakibul Islam, QA Automation Engineer & SDET (Dhaka, remote, UTC+6).
Live at https://rakibul-portfolio.pages.dev.

**Who visits, and what they must leave with**

| Visitor | Arrives from | Must leave knowing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter or hiring manager (often Europe) | CV, LinkedIn, job application | He builds real test automation (Playwright, CI, API), it is proven, and how to contact him |
| Engineering lead reviewing a candidate | A shared link | How he thinks about test strategy, from case studies and the site's own QA report |
| Peers | LinkedIn posts, GitHub | His writing and tools |

**The one main action:** email him (or download the CV).

**Mode (Impeccable):** Experience. The work leads; the interface recedes.

**Product truths (never invent around these)**
- Facts come only from `src/content/site.json` and the Markdown in `src/content/`.
- The site is its own proof: every push runs the full test suite, and `/qa` shows the real results.
- Missing facts are lorem ipsum with a `<!-- TODO -->` and `placeholder: true`, never made-up numbers.
